# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## Our standard

Be respectful and constructive. Welcome newcomers, assume good intent, and focus feedback on the work rather than the person.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and contributions that violate this code, and may block participants who do. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through the email address on the GitHub profile [@Nithinfgs](https://github.com/Nithinfgs). Reports are handled privately.
